# My Dream Home — Minimal Interactive 3D House

A compact, north-facing 3-level Indian home visualizer built with Three.js.

## Floor arrangement

- **Ground floor:** enclosed storage, cow and buffalo shed, calf pen, gated grass paddock, manger, water trough, wash / dairy utility, drainage, fodder space, manure / compost bay, planting and aligned stairs.
- **Second floor — 3BHK:** master bedroom with attached toilet, two bedrooms, hall / living, dining, kitchen, common toilet, pooja room and north balcony.
- **Third floor — 2BHK:** two bedrooms, hall / living, family lounge, dining, kitchen, attached and common toilets, pooja room and terrace.

## Architectural features

- North-facing entry and aligned north compound gate / approach.
- Vastu-inspired room zoning.
- A pitched metal roof over the open-sided cattle shed.
- A separate flat roof terrace with parapets, pergola, solar panels, overhead tank and rainwater downpipe.
- The residential floors share a coordinated stair core.
- Roof can be shown or hidden to inspect the upper floor.

## Controls

- Select all floors, ground, second (3BHK) or third (2BHK).
- Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom.
- Use room chips to move the camera to bedrooms, hall, kitchen, toilets and utility spaces.
- Shift + mouse wheel or Up / Down / Page Up / Page Down to change floors.
- Toggle floor plan, roof, room tour, rotation, zoom and camera reset.
- Responsive controls for mobile and desktop.

## Orientation

North is front / +Z. East is right / +X. South is rear / -Z. West is left / -X.

## Technology

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Three.js and OrbitControls. Three.js modules load from jsDelivr.

## Important

This is an illustrative 3D design, not a construction drawing. Dimensions, animal capacity, ventilation, drainage, structure, roof loads, water tank support, setbacks and building approvals must be checked by qualified local professionals against the actual surveyed plot and current local rules.
